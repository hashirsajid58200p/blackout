## Brand & Style

This design system treats the interface not as ephemeral software, but as a dedicated physical instrument—specifically a convergence of an admiralty chronometer, an official shipping logbook, and an indelible government register. It serves as an uncompromising, authoritative utility for digital restriction and deliberate focus.

The emotional target is solemn, irrevocable discipline. It communicates gravity, permanence, and calm mechanical certainty rather than modern gamified productivity. Interactions evoke the feeling of filling out an unalterable customs declaration or stamping an iron-bound register.

### Core Philosophy & Strict Prohibitions
- **Material Honesty:** Surfaces represent tactile record-keeping artifacts: cold-pressed slate paper in light environments and heavy, oil-tanned dark naval leather in dark environments.
- **Zero Graphic Device Policy:** No logos, vector glyphs, mascot illustrations, monograms, or decorative abstract icons are permitted anywhere. The title text is rendered purely in unadorned, tracked typographic caps.
- **Atmospheric Discipline:** Banished entirely are dropshadows, ambient light halos, specular highlights, glassmorphism, blurs, bouncy spring easings, and fluid organic blobs.
- **Angular Rigidity:** The maximum allowed corner radius across the entire system is strictly 2px. All controls, borders, entry wells, and cards remain structurally rectilinear.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from archival document materials: oxidized carbon inks, bleached slate ledger paper, maritime bindings, brass calibrations, and scarlet endorsement ink.

### Dark Mode (Primary Default)
- **Canvas Base (`#12161F`):** Deepest near-black navy leather cover. Serves as the global backdrop.
- **Surface / Plate (`#1B2030`):** Recessed ledger panel and card container surface.
- **Primary Text / Marks (`#E6E8EC`):** Bleached bone-white letterpress imprint.
- **Secondary Text / Annotations (`#8C93A6`):** Slate-gray secondary ledger notations.
- **Hairlines & Dividers (`#2A3145`):** 1px structural ink lines and rule matrices.

### Light Mode (Ledger Paper)
- **Canvas Base (`#E6E8EC`):** Uncoated cool slate ledger stock.
- **Surface / Plate (`#EFF1F4`):** Crisp archival page sheet.
- **Primary Ink (`#1A2030`):** Heavy dark navy logbook ink.
- **Secondary Ink (`#5C6478`):** Faded carbon notation ink.
- **Hairlines & Dividers (`#C9CDD6`):** Machine-ruled paper guidelines.

### Functional Accents
- **Locked / Restricted / Warning (`#B23A2E`):** Matted iron-oxide stamp red. Used for active blockades, irreversible lockouts, and penalty timers. Never illuminated; acts like quick-drying official endorsement ink.
- **Unlocked / Permitted / Calibration (`#A67C3D`):** Burnished maritime instrument brass. Used for confirmed allowances, chronometer calibrations, and structural status highlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct operational voices:

1. **The Official Record (Display & Headlines):** Set in `Libre Caslon Text`. Used exclusively for document titles, modal headers, major state declarations, and formal status headings. It provides historical, authoritative presence.
2. **The Field Manual (Body, Controls, Metas):** Set in `Public Sans`. Rational, objective, and starkly legible at small scales. All Section Headers are forced uppercase with `letter-spacing: 0.12em` at 11px to evoke ledger ledger-book index classifications.
3. **The Chronometer & Manifest (Numerics & Timers):** Set in `IBM Plex Mono` (or standard monospaced tabular figures). All countdowns, timestamps, session duration counters, statistical tallies, and serial-number styled IDs must use this monospace voice to maintain mechanical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a tight, tabular ledger grid. Screen space is divided by ruling lines rather than loose floating cards.

- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column structure on mobile devices, expanding to 8 columns on tablets. Fixed 16px outer margin (`1rem`) on handheld devices with dense 12px gutters (`0.75rem`).
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content proceeds down the page in strictly measured ledger tiers separated by hairline borders. Whitespace is purposeful, compact, and never loose or floating.
- **Dividers:** Horizontal and vertical separations rely on continuous 1px rules or perforated dashed patterns (`border-style: dashed; border-width: 1px 0 0 0; stroke-dasharray: 3 3;`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through mechanical registration and flat surface demarcation rather than physical height above the canvas.

- **Absolute Prohibition of Shadows:** Box shadows, drop shadows, and elevation blurs are strictly forbidden (`box-shadow: none; filter: none;`).
- **Tonal Insets & Hairline Boundaries:** Depth is conveyed entirely through background contrast transitions bounded by 1px solid hairlines (`#2A3145` in dark mode, `#C9CDD6` in light mode).
- **Recessed Ledger Wells:** When an area requires nesting (such as an active lockout monitor or strict app list), the interior plate drops to a slightly shifted tone with an inner 1px border.
- **Physical Stamp Overlay:** Important notifications or hard warnings do not appear on floating toast bubbles. They are rendered as rigid, rectangular ledger blocks stamped into the layout flow with ink-red boundaries (`#B23A2E`).

## Shapes

All shapes reflect sheet metal, card stock, and trimmed paper slips.
- Global base border-radius is set to strictly `0px` by default.
- Where slight tactile softenings are necessary for native Android touch feedback, radius may not exceed `2px` (`border-radius: 2px`).
- Circular avatars, rounded pills, capsule tags, and organic bubble contours are strictly non-compliant.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Slips
- **Primary Lock / Affirm Button:** Solid rectangle with `border-radius: 0px` (or maximum `2px`), bounded by a 1px solid rule. In dark mode, features a solid `#E6E8EC` ground with `#12161F` uppercase bold `Public Sans` text. Focus/Active states invert sharply without transition animations.
- **Secondary / Manifest Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid hairline (`#2A3145` dark / `#C9CDD6` light), text in uppercase `Public Sans` tracking `0.1em`.
- **Destructive / Irrevocable Emergency Action:** Transparent or `#1B2030` ground, bounded by a 1px solid `#B23A2E` border with `#B23A2E` monospace or uppercase text.
- No rounded buttons, no pill shapes, no icons within buttons.

### Chronometer & Lockout Displays
- **Timer Gauge:** Displays remain strictly numeric, utilizing `IBM Plex Mono` display sizing. Timers are framed within a 1px hairline border well labeled with a top-mounted `section-header` tag (e.g., `REMAINING DURATION // REF: 884-A`).
- Time divisions are separated by raw typography (`:` or `.`).

### Lists & Manifest Ledgers
- List items (such as blocked applications or restriction rules) are stacked rows separated by 1px hairlines.
- Each row contains plain typographic information: the application package or clear text label on the left in `Public Sans` body, and operational status or session rule on the right in `IBM Plex Mono` (e.g., `LOCKED [04:20:00]` or `PERMITTED`).
- Application icons are deliberately omitted. Applications are recognized strictly by their human-readable title and technical identifier.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat rectangular field well with a 1px hairline perimeter.
- Focus state replaces the hairline with a 1px `#A67C3D` (brass) or `#E6E8EC` rule. No halo, no soft focus rings.
- Placeholder text is set in secondary ink color with monospaced style.

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- **Checkboxes:** Rigid 14px × 14px squares with a 1px border. Checked state renders a solid filled inner block (leaving a 2px air gap) or an austere typographic `X` in monospace font.
- **Radio Buttons:** Square outer framing with a centered square core. Circular radio discs are prohibited to preserve the drafting aesthetic.
- **Toggles / Switches:** No iOS-style rounded track switches. Replaced by a segmented mechanical bracket: `[ OFF | ON ]` where the active state receives inverted fill and contrast text.

### Perforated Cards & Stamped Notices
- System cards feature an optional perforated top or bottom divider (`border-top: 1px dashed`).
- Notice placards for ongoing hard locks feature a framed stamp motif: double 1px hairlines enclosing the status declaration set in uppercase `Libre Caslon Text` or tracked `Public Sans` with `#B23A2E` ink coloring.
